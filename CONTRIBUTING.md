# Contributing

## Publishing an operational area

Operators should use the **Publish Operational Area** issue form rather than directly editing another operator's data.

For the POC:

1. Open **Issues → New issue**.
2. Select **Publish Operational Area**.
3. Provide the required metadata.
4. Provide the GeoJSON Feature.
5. Submit the request.
6. A maintainer reviews the proposed change.
7. Automated validation and overlap checks run.
8. The approved change is merged.

## Data principle

Only publish the minimum information needed for operational-area awareness.

Do not include detailed flight plans, routes, customers, volumes, or other commercially sensitive operational information.
